How it works

Your neighbourhood is the game board

🌍

Choose a world

Halloween, Fantasy, Anime or The Other Side. Each has its own creatures, colours and music.

📡

Follow the radar

The map shows who is lurking nearby. Walk towards them on foot – they hide while you ride.

👁️

Meet them in AR

Raise your phone and see them standing on your street. Tap a creature to collect a star.

👥

Hunt together

Everyone nearby sees the same creatures in the same places. Go out with friends.

Worlds

Pick your world every time you go out

Each world recolours the camera image with its own look and fills your streets with its own cast.

HalloweenGolden dusk, glowing pumpkins, bats and restless spirits
FantasyA warm fairy-tale autumn of elves, dwarves, leprechauns and trolls
AnimeA cel-shaded world of heroes, ninjas and cute critters
The Other SideA cold blue world under a burning red sky

🧩 Bring your own monsters: load any downloadable 3D model from Sketchfab and meet it at the next corner.

Early access

Join the test on Google Play

LurkGo is in closed testing on Google Play. Testers get every update first and help decide what comes next.

  1. Ask for an access codeWrite to us from the Google account you use on your phone. We reply with a code, usually within a day.
  2. Open the testing linkSign in with the same Google account and tap “Become a tester”.
  3. Install from Google PlayFollow the “Download it on Google Play” link on that page. Updates arrive like for any other app.
Request an access code Open the testing link

Email doesn't open? Write to yougosupport@gmail.com with the subject “LurkGo access code”.

Why it matters: Google Play opens a new app to everyone only after enough testers have played it for two weeks. Every tester brings the public release closer.
